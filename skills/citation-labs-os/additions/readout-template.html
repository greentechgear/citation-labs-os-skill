Findings

Security claims are cited to third parties

ObservationOBSERVED On P1 and P3, certification claims about the product cite an analyst blog and a distributor page, not the company.
"…certified to IEC 62443-4-2 according to a 2024 distributor listing…"
InterpretationINFERRED The company's certificate evidence is fragmented across PDFs, so systems may prefer pages that state it in one place. Retrieval of company pages is UNKNOWN.
Proposed actionRead the cited distributor page to see what it actually supports, then check whether a company page states the certificate scope at all.

Version 3 firmware is misdescribed

ObservationOBSERVED ChatGPT P4 says signed firmware arrived in version 4.
InterpretationHYPOTHESIS Release notes do not state version conditions plainly.
Proposed actionConfirm the true version history with product engineering before treating this as an accuracy error.
